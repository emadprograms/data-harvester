# Quick Task 260926-e4s: US Eastern Timezone (NYSE Stock Exchange Time) for Historical Database Charts

## Description
Convert timestamps displayed in the charts and related inspectors on the Historical Database page (`tab-charts`) to US Eastern Timezone (`America/New_York`), matching NYSE stock exchange market hours (09:30 market open, 16:00 market close, pre-market, and post-market).

## User Requirements & Architecture Context
- **Why US / NYSE Timezone:**
  - Historical database market data consists of US equities and ETFs (SPY, QQQ, NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, AMD, AMZN, etc.) ingested from Polygon/Massive.
  - Traders, quants, and market operators evaluate market sessions based on NYSE exchange local time (`America/New_York`: EST UTC-5 / EDT UTC-4).
  - Previously, timestamps in DuckDB were queried as UTC, causing regular session open to render at 13:30 or 14:30 and post-market close to render at 23:59 or 00:59, introducing confusion with respect to NYSE market hours.
- **Scope of Changes:**
  - **Backend Analytics Engine (`src/dashboard/analytics.py`):**
    - Convert `timestamp` in DuckDB queries for `get_historical_candles` and `get_streaming_candles` to US Eastern timezone: `((timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')`.
    - Calculate epoch and `time_str` based on NYSE local clock so dynamic `time_bucket` (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1d) aligns exactly with NYSE trading days and hours.
    - Return `"timezone": "America/New_York"` metadata in the candle payload.
  - **Chart Engine (`src/dashboard/static/js/chart.js`):**
    - Configure Lightweight Charts `localization.timeFormatter` to display crosshair time in NYSE time (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm ET`).
    - Configure Lightweight Charts `timeScale.tickMarkFormatter` to format time-axis tick marks cleanly in NYSE hours (`HH:mm`), days (`MMM D`), months (`MMM`), and years (`YYYY`).
    - Update interactive hover legend to display timestamps with `ET` suffix (e.g. `2024-10-01 09:30:00 ET`).
    - Update notice banner to explicitly mention US Eastern Time (NYSE: EST/EDT).
  - **Table Inspector & UI (`src/dashboard/static/js/tables.js`, `src/dashboard/static/index.html`):**
    - Add `NYSE (ET)` badge to the chart legend bar.
    - Change table header from `Timestamp (UTC)` to `Timestamp (US/Eastern)`.
    - Update raw candle inspector row formatting and CSV export to reflect US Eastern Time.
  - **Verification:**
    - Dedicated test suite `tests/dashboard/test_chart_timezone.py` verifying market open (09:30) and close (16:00) timestamps for regular session data.
    - Full pytest suite verification.
