# Quick Task 260925-wf0: Databento Tick Backfill for streaming.duckdb

## Description
Backfill `data/streaming.duckdb` (`ticks` table) with tick-by-tick TBBO (Trade and Top-of-Book Quotes) using Databento. Process iteratively one trading day at a time going backward from the most recent session, strictly targeting single-stock symbols (excluding all ETFs, crypto, and commodities) during the last 30 minutes of premarket (09:00–09:30 ET) and regular market hours (09:30–16:00 ET), while tracking cost per day to respect the $125 credit budget.

## User Requirements & Architecture Context
- **API Provider:** Databento (`DBEQ.BASIC` dataset, `tbbo` schema).
- **Asset Filtering:** Strictly single-stock symbols from `symbol_map` (19 symbols: AAPL, ADBE, AMD, AMZN, APP, AVGO, BABA, GOOGL, META, MSFT, MU, NDAQ, NVDA, ORCL, PANW, QCOM, SHOP, TSLA, TSM). Exclude all ETFs (SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, XLE, XLF, XLK, XLC, XLI, XLP, XLU, XLV, TLT, SMH, SOXX), crypto (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, EURUSDT, PAXGUSDT), and commodities (CL=F, GC=F, VIX, UUP).
- **Session Hours:** Exactly 09:00:00 ET to 16:00:00 ET per trading day (last 30 minutes of premarket + regular hours).
- **Execution Model:** Day-by-day sequential loop going backwards (T-1, T-2, T-3...).
  - Check `get_cost()` before fetching each day.
  - Verify cumulative cost against $125 budget limit.
  - Fetch day's tick data, map to `ticks` table schema, and insert into `streaming.duckdb`.
  - Log progress, record counts, and running cost balance.
- **Table Schema:** `ticks` in `streaming.duckdb`:
  `(timestamp TIMESTAMP, symbol VARCHAR, price DOUBLE, volume DOUBLE, bid DOUBLE, ask DOUBLE, source VARCHAR, session VARCHAR)` with `source='DATABENTO'`.
