### Guard added — the chart's missing-data visualisation (owner clarification, 2026-10-05)

The owner asked what "bar subsystem" means in Phase 47, because the shaded
regions showing where data is missing are wanted. Those are two different
things, and the plan now says so explicitly:

| | Removed in Phase 47 | Retained |
|---|---|---|
| What | The historical **1-minute bar archive pipeline** — Massive/Polygon/Yahoo/Binance harvesters writing `minute_data` in `historical.duckdb`, their CLI and dashboard job | The tick-lake **gap visualisation** — shaded missing-data regions, continuity ribbons, quiet-interval analysis |
| Where | `main.py` harvest CLI, `src/data/harvester.py`, `normalizer.py`, `src/api/{massive,yahoo,binance}.py`, `tools/{backfill_massive,benchmark_baseline,audit_database_integrity}.py`, `src/dashboard/harvester_job.py` | `src/dashboard/static/js/chart.js` (`GapShadingRenderer`/`GapShadingPaneView`/`GapShadingPlugin`), `index.html` continuity markup, `src/utils/integrity.py::detect_stream_quiet_intervals`, `/api/streaming/continuity[/weeks]` |

`tests/dashboard/test_gap_visualisation_survives.py` (5 tests) locks the retained
stack in: the plugin is defined and attached to the streaming candle series; a
lake with a deliberate 19-minute session hole returns non-empty `gaps` from
`/api/candles` (the end-to-end path from ticks to shading); the quiet-interval
analysis exists and is lake-backed; the continuity ribbon markup and routes
survive. Requirement wording (RMV-02, RMV-03) and the Phase 47 goal now name both
sides explicitly.
